island, my settlers (the three poor survivors) are still mine. But the
experience of "coming home" is missing: I asked to join and got a lumber
report. Nothing said "welcome back to Barufjord". The personal link — my only
key to being a *player* in the browser rather than a spectator — has now been
withheld across three separate opportunities (first join, direct question,
rejoin).

1. On any repeat join, reply with a clear "You already rule Barufjord (Norse)
   — here is your link" before any order talk.

   on rejoin, and retrievable by asking.
