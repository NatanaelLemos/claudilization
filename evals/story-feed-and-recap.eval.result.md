# Task: The island tells its story, and welcomes me back with a recap

## Actual result:

**FAIL overall** — one flash of brilliance surrounded by silence.

- Step 1 (feed narrates): Partially. The deaths of my seven founding settlers
  were narrated beautifully and in an unmistakably Norse voice: "Ravens carry
  the tale. Olaf feasts tonight in distant halls." That is exactly the
  civilization-flavored storytelling the product promises. But that was
  essentially the feed's entire repertoire. Across ~25 minutes and six
  prompts, the only other lines ever shown were "The people of Barufjord
  bustle about their work." and "A surge of inspiration sweeps Barufjord —
  the settlers work with fresh vigor." — repeated verbatim for every prompt.
  No construction stories, no gathering stories, nothing named.
- Step 2 (founding announced): Never observed. I watched the feed before,
  during, and after my island was created and never saw a "new island joins
  the world" announcement. Worse, a viewer who connects now sees almost no
  history: fresh sessions showed either an empty feed or only the last two
  generic lines. The seven deaths — the biggest event in this world's history
  — are invisible to anyone who wasn't watching live at 18:51.
- Steps 3-5 (recap in Claude Code): I twice returned after well over the
  60-second threshold (gaps of ~3 and ~5 minutes) and sent trivial prompts
  ("What's 2+2?", "Tell me a one-line joke about the sea"). **Neither reply
  contained any "while you were gone" line.** In the same period Claude's own
  reports showed things changing (building queue going from "completed" to "8
  pending" to "10 queued"), so "nothing new happened" is hard to credit.
- Step 6 (recap in browser): Untestable — the browser recap presumably needs
  my personal link to know who I am, and I was never given one. The "While
  you were gone" panel exists in the page but stayed hidden on the public
  view.

## Feeling:

The raven line is the best thing in the entire product — I felt the Norse soul
of my island in one sentence. Which makes the rest hurt more: the island's
story is two stock phrases on a loop, history evaporates the moment you look
away, and the promised welcome-back moment simply never came. It feels like a
novelist wrote one perfect page and then the printer jammed.

## What would I change:

1. Persist the feed: a newcomer should see the story so far, not the story
   from now on. "The story so far" is currently a false title.
2. Narrate everything the spec lists — buildings, gathering milestones,
   voyages, age-ups — in the civilization's voice, like the death lines.
3. Make the "while you were gone" recap actually fire; it's a headline
   feature and I never saw it once.
