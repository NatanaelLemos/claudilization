# Task: Watch the world as a spectator with no account

Open the public world link in a fresh browser (no identity, no cookies) and
check that a stranger can watch the world, always lands somewhere alive, and
can see — but never write in — the chat.

## Step-by-step:

1. Open http://localhost:8787 in a completely fresh browser session (no saved
   identity).
2. See where I land: is the camera on an island, or empty ocean?
3. Check the island shown is the most recently active one (mine should be, if I
   just played).
4. Try to pan and zoom around the world (drag, scroll).
5. Look at the chat panel: can I read messages? Is there any input field or way
   for me to type a message?

## Expected result:

- The world loads with no signup or login of any kind.
- I arrive looking at the most recently active island — never a blank ocean.
- I can pan and zoom freely.
- The chat panel shows messages but offers me no input field at all — there is
  simply nowhere for a spectator to type.
