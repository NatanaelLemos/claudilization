# Task: Join the world and see my brand-new Stone Age island

## Actual result:

**MIXED — leaning FAIL.** Joining worked in one step, but the welcome was
broken in two important ways and the island suffered a catastrophe before I
even saw it.

- Step 1-2: I typed "Please join claudilization as the norse civilization".
  About two minutes later I got a reply. It did name my island — **Barufjord**,
  a lovely Norse name — but it contained **no personal link at all**. It read
  like a status report: "Barufjord's settlers are now working: 5 gathering
  food (food stock was critically low), 4 on wood, 1 on stone..." No welcome,
  no "here is your island", no link. I later asked outright "What is my
  island's personal link?" and got back "Orders delivered — good to go on your
  end. Anything else you need?" — my question was completely ignored.
- Step 3-4: With no personal link, I opened the public link instead. The
  camera was centered on Barufjord (the only island), HUD showing "Barufjord —
  Stone Age · Norse". That part looked right.
- Step 5: **I could not see a single settler.** I zoomed in, panned, orbited
  overhead, and took screenshots seconds apart: trees, rocks, berry bushes —
  no people, no name labels, no movement, ever. I know the 10 settlers existed
  and had unique Norse names (Olaf, Sigrid, Sven, Embla, Harald, Erik, Knut,
  Freya, Torstein, Ylva) only because the story feed and Claude's replies
  mentioned them — never because I saw one.
- Step 6: Nature was right: pine trees, grey rock deposits, yellow berry
  bushes, sandy beach, no buildings. Genuinely pretty.
- The catastrophe: while my join prompt was still processing, the story feed
  announced seven deaths — "Ravens carry the tale. Olaf feasts tonight in
  distant halls." (and six more). My island started with zero food and nobody
  gathering, and by the time my own join reply arrived, 7 of my 10 settlers
  had starved. My very first minute as a player killed most of my people.

## Feeling:

Whiplash. The island name and the Norse death poetry are genuinely evocative —
"feasts tonight in distant halls" gave me chills. But I joined, and before I
could even find my island, seven settlers were dead through no action of mine.
That felt unfair and alienating, like being handed a funeral instead of a
founding. And not getting my personal link — then being brushed off when I
asked for it — made me feel locked out of my own island.

## What would I change:

1. A new island must survive its own birth: give it starting food or have
   settlers forage by default so nobody can die before the player's first
   reply arrives.
2. Always include the personal link in the join reply, and answer when the
   player asks for it.
3. Draw the settlers! Ten little figures with names is the whole promise of
   "your island is alive". Right now it's a beautiful diorama with no one in
   it.
