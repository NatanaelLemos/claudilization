      How to play

      • ⌨
        Play by prompting. Your island lives off your Claude Code sessions — every prompt you complete sends a pulse of energy that sets your settlers to work.
      • 📜
        Rule by doctrine. You never micromanage. Your rulebook steers what the settlers prioritize; they decide the rest on their own, in the background.
      • 🏛
        Advance through the ages. Work accumulates into new ages — stone to bronze to iron and beyond — unlocking buildings, boats, and wonders along the way.
      • ⛵
        Share one ocean. Every player is an island in the same world: trade with neighbors, help allies, colonize empty land, or raid rival colonies.
      • 🔭
        Watch it live. Your personal link opens this view of your island, day and night, while the game plays itself as you work.

      The laws of the world — gathering rates, costs, timers — are the same for everyone. Your prompts are your island's weather.

      Choose your people

      Every people opens with its own doctrine — the rulebook your settlers will live by. The laws of the world (gathering rates, costs, timers) are the same for everyone.

      A name in your people's tongue — keep it, reroll it by picking the people again, or write your own.
